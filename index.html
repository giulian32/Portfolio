One loop, kept tight: click to earn, spend on upgrades, rebirth for bigger multipliers. The moon holds rare lunar mutations, the cookie catalogue runs from Sparkle to Rainbow to simply Huge, and your inventory decides whether a find gets sold or kept. Bring two friends and the game hands you a Rainbow Cookie.

In the game now

  • 01Click-to-earn loopCurrency, upgrades and a rebirth system for compounding multipliers.
  • 02Moon travelFarm rare lunar mutations you can’t get from ordinary clicks.
  • 03Cookie catalogueSparkle, Rainbow and Huge variants, each with its own rarity tier.
  • 04InventorySell a find, stash it, or drop it for other players.
  • 05Invite rewardsTwo friends in, one Rainbow Cookie out.
